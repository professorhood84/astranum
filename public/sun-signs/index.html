Sun sign guide: dates, traits & meanings

Your Sun sign is the zodiac sign the Sun was passing through on the day you were born — the foundation of your astrology profile and the sign most people mean when they ask "what's your sign?" Find yours below.

Element & modality, in short

Every sign has an element — Fire, Earth, Air, or Water — that describes its core temperament (action, practicality, ideas, or feeling), and a modality — Cardinal, Fixed, or Mutable — that describes how it moves: Cardinal signs initiate, Fixed signs sustain, and Mutable signs adapt. Your Sun sign combines one of each.

Aries

March 21 – April 19

Fire · Cardinal · Ruled by Mars

Aries opens the zodiac year with pure momentum — bold, competitive, and quick to act on instinct rather than wait for the perfect plan. This is the sign most likely to be first, whatever "first" happens to mean that day.

Strengths

Courageous, energetic, direct, a natural initiator.

Watch-outs

Can be impatient, impulsive, or quick to anger.

♉

Taurus

April 20 – May 20

Earth · Fixed · Ruled by Venus

Taurus values what's real and lasting — good food, comfort, steady routines, and relationships built slowly over time. Change doesn't come easily here, but once a Taurus commits, they rarely waver.

Strengths

Grounded, patient, loyal, sensual, dependable.

Watch-outs

Can be stubborn, possessive, or resistant to necessary change.

♊

Gemini

May 21 – June 20

Air · Mutable · Ruled by Mercury

Gemini's mind moves fast, jumping between ideas, conversations, and interests with genuine curiosity. Quick-witted and endlessly communicative, this sign keeps things interesting wherever it goes.

Strengths

Curious, adaptable, witty, an excellent communicator.

Watch-outs

Can be scattered, inconsistent, or prone to restlessness.

♋

Cancer

June 21 – July 22

Water · Cardinal · Ruled by the Moon

Cancer feels things deeply and protects the people it loves fiercely. Home, family, and emotional security matter enormously here — this sign builds a nest wherever it lands, literally or figuratively.

Strengths

Nurturing, intuitive, loyal, deeply empathetic.

Watch-outs

Can be moody, overly guarded, or prone to taking things personally.

♌

Leo

July 23 – August 22

Fire · Fixed · Ruled by the Sun

Leo carries natural warmth and presence — a sign that lights up a room without necessarily trying to. Generous and loyal to the people in their circle, Leos want to be seen, but they also want to make others feel seen.

Strengths

Confident, generous, warm, a natural performer and leader.

Watch-outs

Can be prideful, attention-seeking, or resistant to criticism.

♍

Virgo

August 23 – September 22

Earth · Mutable · Ruled by Mercury

Virgo notices what everyone else misses and quietly works to make things better, more efficient, or more correct. Practical and detail-oriented, this sign shows love through useful action more than grand gestures.

Strengths

Analytical, meticulous, helpful, reliably practical.

Watch-outs

Can be overly critical, perfectionistic, or prone to overthinking.

♎

Libra

September 23 – October 22

Air · Cardinal · Ruled by Venus

Libra seeks balance and fairness almost instinctively, weighing every side of a situation before settling anywhere. Socially graceful and drawn to beauty and harmony, this sign is a natural diplomat.

Strengths

Diplomatic, fair-minded, charming, genuinely collaborative.

Watch-outs

Can be indecisive, conflict-avoidant, or overly focused on others' opinions.

♏

Scorpio

October 23 – November 21

Water · Fixed · Ruled by Mars & Pluto

Scorpio feels everything at full intensity and rarely does anything halfway. Perceptive and strategic, this sign is drawn to what's hidden beneath the surface — and is fiercely loyal once trust is earned.

Strengths

Passionate, resourceful, loyal, deeply perceptive.

Watch-outs

Can be secretive, jealous, or prone to holding grudges.

♐

Sagittarius

November 22 – December 21

Fire · Mutable · Ruled by Jupiter

Sagittarius chases the bigger picture — new places, new philosophies, new horizons. Optimistic and blunt in equal measure, this sign would rather have an honest, expansive life than a small, careful one.

Strengths

Adventurous, optimistic, honest, big-picture thinker.

Watch-outs

Can be tactless, restless, or commitment-averse.

♑

Capricorn

December 22 – January 19

Earth · Cardinal · Ruled by Saturn

Capricorn plays the long game, building status and stability through discipline rather than luck. Ambitious but patient, this sign takes real satisfaction in earning something the hard, right way.

Strengths

Disciplined, responsible, ambitious, patient under pressure.

Watch-outs

Can be overly serious, guarded, or workaholic.

♒

Aquarius

January 20 – February 18

Air · Fixed · Ruled by Saturn & Uranus

Aquarius thinks ahead of the room, drawn to unconventional ideas and bigger causes rather than personal comfort. Independent and idea-driven, this sign values authenticity over fitting in.

Strengths

Innovative, independent, humanitarian, refreshingly original.

Watch-outs

Can be emotionally detached, stubborn, or contrarian for its own sake.

♓

Pisces

February 19 – March 20

Water · Mutable · Ruled by Jupiter & Neptune

Pisces closes the zodiac year with deep empathy and imagination, often absorbing the moods of everyone around it. Artistic and intuitive, this sign moves fluidly between the practical world and its own inner one.

Strengths

Compassionate, imaginative, intuitive, artistically gifted.

Watch-outs

Can be escapist, overly idealistic, or prone to absorbing others' emotions.
